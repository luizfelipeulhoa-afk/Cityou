# Prompts das decorações da ilha (Nano Banana 2 Pro)

Seguem `docs/DESIGN-SYSTEM.md`: bloco de estilo, bloco de materiais da ilha e vista final, nesta ordem. Vista principal: proporção 1:1, 2K. Depois, folha de 4 vistas (template do design system, trocando "building" por "object") e recorte. No Tripo: face_limit 3.000 para peças 1x1, 4.000 para 2x2, 5.000 para 3x3; pbr desligado. Ids sugeridos para o catálogo: `island-<nome>`.

## Rede de descansar (1x1) — `island-hammock`

```
Stylized low-poly 3D game asset for a mobile game. Faceted, flat-shaded polygons with visible planar faces, chunky toy-diorama proportions, simple bold readable silhouette. No text, no letters, no signs, no thin wires, no tiny details. Limited palette only: grass green #80d06e, dark green #4ea477, sand #f7e4ab, sea teal #56dad4, sky blue #a6dcff, cream #f6efdf, terracotta #D9644A, warm wood brown #B07A4F, stone gray #a99f98, sunny yellow #ffd166, coral #ea7651, deep navy #2E3A59. The object stands on a thin round 10-sided low-poly base disk, flat sand top with brown earth sides, diameter just larger than the object footprint. Soft, even, neutral studio lighting, no cast shadows, no specular highlights, no glow, no ambient occlusion, matte surfaces. Pure white seamless background, single object, fully visible and centered with generous margin.

Built ONLY from materials found on a small tropical island: rough palm-tree logs, bamboo poles, woven palm-leaf thatch, coconut-fiber rope lashings, rounded gray volcanic stones, driftwood planks, seashells and coral pieces as decoration. Nothing industrial: no metal, no glass, no bricks, no concrete, no nails, no plastic.

Subject: a relaxing hammock tied between two short leaning palm trunks, the trunks with a few chunky dark-green fronds on top. The hammock is a wide sagging cream-colored woven fabric with two thick terracotta stripes, rope lashings at both ends, one small coconut on the sand beside it.

Three-quarter isometric view, camera 30 degrees above, front-right corner facing the viewer.
```
